## Full Summary

Implements and evaluates an entropy-adaptive background job scheduler that uses Shannon entropy of CPU load to dynamically adjust scheduling thresholds, protecting foreground task latency during bursty periods. Compares four scheduler variants (Baseline fixed-55%, Entropy-only, Momentum-only, Entropy-Adaptive) on 10 trials of synthetic workloads with Poisson arrivals and spike patterns. Measures completion time coefficient of variation (CV), foreground p95 latency, entropy-outcome correlation, false positive rate, and statistical significance via paired t-tests and Cohen's d effect sizes. Outputs: method_out.json with per-scheduler metrics and paired comparisons; full/mini/preview variants for analysis.
